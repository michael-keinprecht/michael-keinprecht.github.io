I am a PhD student at the Economics department of the Vienna University of Economics and Business (WU). You can find my CV here.

My research uses experimental methods (lab and online) to answer various questions revolving around fairness, equality and social mobility. I'm especially excited about designs which allow to properly isolate and test/identify a single mechanism, which then helps understanding real world decisions.

Contact
I’m always happy to talk with people working on related questions and I am open to new projects/collaborations, giving as well as receiving feedback and learning new things every day along the way. You can reach out at michael.keinprecht@wu.ac.at.
