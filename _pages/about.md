I am a PhD student in Economics at the Vienna University of Economics and Business (WU). You can find my [CV here](/files/CV_Keinprecht.pdf).

My research uses experimental methods (lab and online) to answer questions about fairness, equality and social mobility among others. I am especially excited about designs that make it possible to isolate and test a single mechanism, which helps us understand real-world decisions.

<h2>Contact</h2>
I am always happy to talk with people working on related questions. I am open to new projects and collaborations, and I like both giving and receiving feedback, since it is how I learn something new every day.
